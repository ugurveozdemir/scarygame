# Vespera: The Loop Tape

## Premise

The Vespera Sleep & Neuro-Rehabilitation Clinic treats insomnia and severe trauma, or so it says. In truth it
erases the traumas, phobias and failing memories of rich clients and pours the erased mental debris into
patients nobody will miss ("neural filtering").

The player is not an intruder. They are **Subject 14**, a patient whose memory has been wiped thirteen times.
Escapees are not killed: a high-frequency light burns away their last 24 hours and they are dropped in the
parking lot for the next "behavioural compliance trial".

The objectives on screen are not a game interface. They are memos the player's previous self typed into the
camcorder's on-screen display just before the last reset. The player follows the breadcrumbs of who they
were yesterday, and the clinic has known about it all along: the route the memos describe has become the trial.

## Threats

| Threat | Where | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| The Sentinel (the masked surgeon) | Ground floor, basement, later the grounds | A former staff member wiped down to "patrol and punish". Hears footsteps, chases on sight, checks hiding spots |
| The Remnant | Basement, after the power is cut | A patient ruined by electromagnetic experiments. Invisible to the naked eye, seen only through night vision. Drains the camcorder's battery and breaks up its picture when close |
| Sleepwalkers | Second floor (the sleep ward) | Blindfolded patients standing still. Blind, but they lunge at any noise: walking near them, running, broken glass underfoot. Sneak past crouched |

## Acts

### Prologue: Trial 14

The player wakes on the asphalt of the parking lot. The camcorder powers on with a red battery and no spares.
Memo: they wiped you again; the gate is locked; get to the basement through the last room on the right.

### Act 1: Ground Zero

1. **The grounds.** The memo points to the trash can on the plaza. Inside: the spare battery the previous self
   hid there and a note warning that the surgeon hears footsteps.
2. **The barred room.** The last room on the right is barred from the inside: it was the previous self's hideout.
   The way in is the east vent from the showers in the north wing.
3. **The nurse station.** The intercom at the nurse station pages the far south rooms, which draws the
   Sentinel away from the north wing.
4. **The hideout.** Inside: tally marks of thirteen trials, a mattress, a note. The stairwell door leads down.

### Act 2: Ghosts in the Archive

1. **The archive.** A new record room off the basement corridor: shelves of MiniDV tapes labelled with the
   player's subject number, Trial 01 to Trial 13. Playing Trial 13 on the monitor shows the previous attempt:
   the service lift's code on a note on its panel, then the Sentinel, a chase and the tape cutting out.
2. **The electrical room.** A new room by the morgue. The service lift has no power until the main breaker is
   thrown. Throwing it bursts the basement lights and frees the Remnant from the morgue's cold chambers.
3. **The lift.** The service lift waits in a lobby on the ground floor. Its doors chime when they open, and the
   Sentinel comes to the sound.

### Act 3: The Silent Ward

A new second floor over the main wing: the sleep ward.

1. **Sleep dormitories** with Sleepwalkers standing between the beds and in the corridor, and broken pill
   bottles on the floor.
2. **The observation room.** Security monitors show the parking lot, the plaza, the hideout and the archive:
   every stop on the memos' route. One shows the observation room itself. A one-way mirror looks into the
   sleep lab where the "donors" lie wired up.
3. **The chief physician's office.** Subject 14's file: the clinic lets the subject leave himself memos, because
   he follows them every time. The gate is the last stage of the trial; the reset happens there. The console
   releases every magnetic lock in the building, and the desk drawer holds the fountain pump room's key.

### Act 4: Fog over the Grounds

Releasing the locks sets off the reset siren. The fire escape outside the second floor's pharmacy opens. The
lamps on the grounds turn red, the fog thickens and the Sentinel goes out to patrol the grounds.

Two ways out:

- **The Loop.** Run for the open gate. A floodlight flares white, the high-frequency whine rises and the
  camcorder's display reads *TRIAL 14 COMPLETE · SUBJECT COMPLIANT · RESETTING*. The player wakes in the
  parking lot again, now as Trial 15, and the first memo is different: it was written by the run they just played.
- **Signal Lost.** Use the key on the fountain's maintenance hatch, climb down into the pump room where the
  clinic's transmitter hums, and tear it out. The memos on the screen break up, and an old drainage tunnel
  leads under the fence to the road outside.
